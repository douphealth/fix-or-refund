# Legal source manifest — 30 October 2026

Primary law and official information underlie *guidance*; no source can verify an individual case without documents. This manifest is for maintenance and auditability, not legal advice.

## Primary legislation

1. **Directive (EU) 2019/771**, especially Articles 10–16: sales-of-goods seller responsibility, repair/replacement and conditional reduction/termination: https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2019/771/oj/eng
2. **Directive (EU) 2024/1799**, especially Article 5, Annex II, and amendments to 2019/771: manufacturer repair obligations apply only to goods/parts subject to applicable Union repairability requirements: https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2024/1799/oj/eng
3. **Greek Law 5317/2026**, national implementing text, as recorded by the Hellenic Parliament (FEK 108 A/10.07.2026): https://www.hellenicparliament.gr/Nomothetiko-Ergo/Anazitisi-Nomothetikou-Ergou?law_id=2fa5316d-9df6-4ff0-8eb9-b479000a6e8f
4. **Greek Law 5317/2026 Article 102**, conditionally three-year limitation in case repair is chosen as statutory remedy. Secondary article permalink to ease navigation; cross-reference the official law: https://www.taxheaven.gr/law/5317/2026/arthro/102
5. **Greek Law 5317/2026 Article 143** sets relevant commencement and temporal application. Secondary article permalink; cross-reference official law: https://www.taxheaven.gr/law/5317/2026/arthro/143
6. **Greek Law 5317/2026 Article 97** concerning manufacturer repair obligations and Annex IV: https://www.taxheaven.gr/law/5317/2026/arthro/97

## Official escalation routes

10. Greek Consumer Ombudsman complaint: https://www.synigoroskatanaloti.gr/el/ypoboli-anaforas — requires complaint generally within **one year from full knowledge** of the harmful act or omission; electronic submission needs Taxisnet.
11. Gov.gr consumer complaint: https://www.gov.gr/ipiresies/polites-kai-kathemerinoteta/kataggelies/kataggelia-katanalote
12. ECC Greece complaint guidance: https://www.eccgreece.gr/about-us/complaint-submission — transaction evidence, previous written consumer-to-trader complaint, signed power of attorney required.
13. European Consumer Centres Network: https://www.eccnet.eu/
14. European Commission ADR list: https://consumer-redress.ec.europa.eu/list-alternative-dispute-resolution-adr-bodies_en
